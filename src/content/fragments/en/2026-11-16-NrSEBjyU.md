Since I started doing business internationally, I haven't kept up with much of what's going on in China. Recently, a friend in China happened to mention that he can now use TikTok there with a VPN, even with a Chinese SIM card in his phone. That really surprised me.

When I was in China, I knew TikTok didn't just check your IP address. It also read the carrier and region information from your SIM card. Even with a VPN, if it detected a Chinese SIM, it still wouldn't let you use the app.

My friend told me it's because Apple changed iOS a few years ago. Apps built with newer SDKs can no longer get real carrier information through the old API, only fixed placeholder values. So TikTok can't read the SIM card's actual region through that API anymore.

I wonder when Android will catch up. It still provides APIs for reading a SIM card's country and carrier. Android is open source, though, so even if Google tightens things up someday, phone makers could technically keep those APIs or add their own in their versions of the system. But if Android stopped providing them, it would at least raise the cost of doing harm. It reminds me of Google's old line: “Don't be evil.”

So my view of TikTok is the same as my view of the Chinese Communist Party. TikTok itself represents the Party's will, of course, and these restrictions on Chinese users are it carrying out the Party's wish to wall off information between China and the outside world.

If one day you suddenly find them doing something good, chances are they were forced to. They never do good things of their own accord.
