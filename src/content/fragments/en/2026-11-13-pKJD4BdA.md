A friend recently told me that their company had been giving everyone very generous AI coding quotas. The company found that features were indeed shipping faster, but the business wasn't growing. So it started reviewing how much quota everyone should get.

I believe similar things are happening in many places around the world.

But is this really a quota problem?

I've seen too many companies devote substantial manpower to building products that are utter garbage, only to end up laying people off. Is it really those employees' fault?\
When the business isn't growing, is the AI coding quota really the problem?

Passing the blame really is easy.

As building things gets easier and easier, judging what's worth building will matter more and more.
